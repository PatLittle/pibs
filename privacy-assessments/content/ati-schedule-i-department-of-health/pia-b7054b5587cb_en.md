# Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP): Phase 3 (2024-25)

## Description of CDCP Phase 3

This Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) assesses the privacy implications of the activities related to Stage 3 of the CDCP including member enrolment, provider participation, claims processing and the contact centre operations.

## Why a Privacy Impact Assessment was completed

As per Section C.2.2.9 of the Treasury Board Directive on Privacy Practices, the CDCP Phase 3 activities meet the following criteria for initiating a PIA when undertaking a new program or activity that will involve the creation, collection, use, disclosure, retention or disposal of personal information for an administrative purpose.

## More information

This PIA examines Health Canada's and Sun Life's collection, use, disclosure, retention and disposal of personal information involved in the associated CDCP Stage 3 activities.

The PIA recommended mitigation actions in several risk areas, including accountability, safeguards, retention and disposition authorities, privacy notices, and updating a Personal Information Bank (PIB).

For more information about this PIA, contact Health Canada by email at [DentalEnquiries-DemandesDentaires@hc-sc.gc.ca](mailto:DentalEnquiries-DemandesDentaires@hc-sc.gc.ca)
