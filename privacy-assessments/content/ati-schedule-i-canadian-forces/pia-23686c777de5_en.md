# On-Site Monitoring and Reporting

## Description

During the COVID-19 pandemic, most federal public servants worked remotely as on-site work was suspended across the Government of Canada. As public health conditions improved, departments began transitioning employees back to workplaces using a hybrid work model. To ensure consistent application of hybrid work across the federal public service, the Treasury Board Secretariat issued the Direction on Prescribed Presence in the Workplace, which requires employees to work on-site a minimum number of days each week.

To support the implementation of this direction, the Department of National Defence (DND) monitors employee on-site attendance. Managers are responsible for overseeing attendance within their teams using approved monitoring methods and tools. In March 2026, to assist managers, DND introduced a centralized monitoring and reporting process that complements manager-level monitoring. Under this process, the department generates monthly reports identifying employees who appear to have been on-site for fewer days than required. These reports are produced using information from existing departmental systems, including human resources records, network connectivity indicators, and building access data (taking into account approved leave, accommodations, and other exceptions).

Reports are provided to designated officials within Level 1 organizations to support oversight and validation. Managers remain responsible for confirming attendance information and addressing potential issues with employees as appropriate. Aggregated data from these reports is used to produce dashboards that support departmental oversight and reporting on workplace presence trends.

## Why a privacy impact assessment was completed

DND’s on-site monitoring and reporting activities involve the collection and use of personal information about employees. As such, and to ensure that the Department adheres to federal privacy requirements, DND elected to conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) in relation to its monitoring and reporting plans. The PIA was completed to assess privacy risks associated with monitoring activities and to ensure that employee privacy is respected.

## Additional information

The PIA identified several potential privacy risks associated with the Department’s on-site monitoring and reporting plans. To mitigate these risks, the Department implemented several measures, including limiting employee-level reporting to cases of low on-site connectivity, restricting access to reports to designated officials with a need to know, and requiring managerial validation before any administrative decisions are taken. Additional safeguards include clear guidance on acceptable uses of the information and the secure storage of reports in protected systems. Risk mitigation measures were implemented during the program’s planning and implementation phases in 2025 and early 2026, prior to full operational rollout.
