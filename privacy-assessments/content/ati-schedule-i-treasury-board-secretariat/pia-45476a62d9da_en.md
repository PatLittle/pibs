# Privacy Impact Assessment Summary for the Government of Canada Workplace Accessibility Passport

## Introduction

In May 2019, the Office of Public Service Accessibility at the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat (TBS) published an [accessibility strategy for the public service of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/publicservice/wellness-inclusion-diversity-public-service/diversity-inclusion-public-service/accessibility-public-service/accessibility-strategy-public-service-toc.html). The strategy, Nothing Without Us, identifies actions for all of government.

The strategy focuses on the built environment, information and communications technology, and employment. It aims to help public servants design and deliver accessible programs and services, and create an accessibility-confident public service.

The strategy aims to ensure that the government is a forward-thinking and inclusive employer. The [Government of Canada Workplace Accessibility Passport](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/publicservice/wellness-inclusion-diversity-public-service/diversity-inclusion-public-service/accessibility-public-service/government-canada-workplace-accessibility-passport.html) (the Passport) was created under the strategy, with the support of the [Centralized Enabling Workplace Fund](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/publicservice/wellness-inclusion-diversity-public-service/diversity-inclusion-public-service/accessibility-public-service/accessibility-strategy-public-service-toc/accessibility-strategy-public-service-employment.html).

The [Passport](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/publicservice/wellness-inclusion-diversity-public-service/diversity-inclusion-public-service/accessibility-public-service/government-canada-workplace-accessibility-passport.html) helps federal public service employees with disabilities get the tools, supports and measures they need to perform at their best and succeed in the workplace. It helps recruit, retain and promote persons with disabilities in the federal public service.

The Passport supports conversations between employees and managers about optimal workplace conditions and any necessary adjustments, including adaptive tools or support measures. Federal employees with disabilities can use the Passport to list the situations where they face one or more barriers, list the barriers that they face in those situations, and list potential solutions to address those barriers. The employee and their manager collaborate to determine effective and appropriate solutions so that the employee can succeed.

Once the employee and manager have signed the Passport, repeated requests for additional documentation or justification are avoided, unless the employee’s circumstances or responsibilities have changed, or more effective solutions can be identified.

The Passport is a voluntary tool that supports diversity and inclusion in the workplace. In 2022, TBS launched the Passport as a Microsoft Word–based document that can be downloaded from the [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/publicservice/wellness-inclusion-diversity-public-service/diversity-inclusion-public-service/accessibility-public-service/government-canada-workplace-accessibility-passport.html) website. TBS is currently working to offer a digital Passport that will be accessible via the TBS Applications Portal (TAP) from the myAccessibleWorkplace application.

This privacy impact assessment assessed both the Word version and the digital application. The new digital application is not meant to replace the existing Word version of the Passport. Both will remain accessible to employees because not all federal organizations use the TBS Applications Portal.

## Why a privacy impact assessment was completed

This assessment was conducted to better understand and mitigate the privacy risks associated with the collection, retention, use and disclosure of personal information by TBS and adopting institutions for both versions of the Passport:

- the Word version of the Passport, which is decentralized (federal organizations collect and hold the information about their own employees, with guidance and support from TBS)
- the application, which is centralized within the TBS Applications Portal

The Passport collects personal information from employees who choose to use the Passport to identify situations and remove barriers in the workplace that may hinder their full and equal participation in society, specifically as part of the GC workforce, including but not limited to:

- general employee information
- the situations, barriers and solutions that the employee enters in the Passport

## Privacy impact assessment risk mitigation strategies

Based upon the information available, the privacy impact assessment identified 14 potential risks to the privacy of personal information related to the collection, retention, use and disclosure of personal information.

The potential risks include:

- no very high risks
- 1 high risk
- 11 medium risks
- 2 low risks
- no very low risks

The high risk is as follows:

1. Unknown status of encryption via channels such as email or Microsoft Teams may result in personal information being transmitted or retained in the clear. This may leave the information vulnerable to unauthorized modification or removal.

Program officials established multiple security and privacy safeguards to protect personal information. Examples of these safeguards include, but are not limited to:

- clearly defined legislative and regulatory authorities for the collection, use, disclosure, and retention of personal information
- ongoing consultation with TBS Access to Information and Privacy (ATIP)
- existing memoranda of understanding with Office of Public Service Accessibility partners such as Office of the Chief Information Officer and Information Management and Technology Division, as well as memoranda of understanding with institutions that adopt the digital application
- extensive use of application design data field constraints to minimize the chances of information being entered incorrectly
- comprehensive instructions and guidance for employees and managers
- well-defined and documented internal privacy breach protocols and procedures within TBS
- a signed Statement of Sensitivity for the myAccessibleWorkplace application on the TBS Applications Portal
- a Security Assessment completed to obtain an Authority to Operate

Potential impacts on the privacy of individuals continue to be properly managed by TBS officials through legal, policy and technical measures geared at the protection of personal information. Recommendations and actions to mitigate risks included in the PIA are expected to reduce privacy risks to a low (or acceptable) level.
