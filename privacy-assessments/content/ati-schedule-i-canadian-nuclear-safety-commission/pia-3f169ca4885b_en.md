6) Description of the program or activity

The mission of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) is to regulate the use
of nuclear energy and materials to protect health, safety, security and the environment,
and to respect Canada’s international commitments on the peaceful use of nuclear energy.

The CNSC has implemented enhanced versions of its IBM Cognos Enterprise Planning
and Business Intelligence (EP/BI) tools, which deliver reliable and integrated financial
and non-financial information in a timely manner and minimize the need for human
intervention and manual analysis. This systems enhancement project is known as the
CNSC Planning, Management, and Reporting System (CPMRS). It provides an IM/IT
solution that enables the CNSC to manage, access and integrate data, and to transform it
into business intelligence for corporate decision-making purposes.

Similar projects, also based on EP/BI tools, are successfully in use in other Government
of Canada departments and agencies.
The CPMRS will be utilized to support the process changes for the CNSC’s Fee Revenue
Management Improvement initiative (FRMI). In time, other initiatives may also be
integrated into the CPMRS but, to date, and for the purposes of this PIA report, the FRMI
is the only related initiative.

Because implementing the CPMRS will involve the collection, use, disclosure and
retention of personal information, the CNSC has initiated a privacy impact assessment
(PIA).

Section 2 - Privacy Impact Assessment Findings

The PIA report, produced according to Treasury Board Secretariat guidelines, examines
the privacy-related impacts of the CPMRS and proposes appropriate mitigation strategies
for the identified privacy risks associated with that system.

In particular, the assessment process identifies moderate risks related to performance
monitoring, notice, use and disclosure. Other privacy-related risks of lesser magnitude are
also highlighted in the report. Recommendations are provided to mitigate all of these
risks.

This report is based on the business model for the CPMRS, as described in detail in
section 2.0, as of the date of the report. To the extent that business model revisions occur,
the PIA will be revisited to ensure that any new privacy issues are appropriately
analyzed.
