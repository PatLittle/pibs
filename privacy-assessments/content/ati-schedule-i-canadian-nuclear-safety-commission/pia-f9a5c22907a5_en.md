6) Description of the program or activity

The mission of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) is to regulate the use
of nuclear energy and materials to protect health, safety, security and the environment,
and to respect Canada’s international commitments on the peaceful use of nuclear energy.

The CNSC’s Harmonized Plan for Improvement Initiatives (HP) is a corporate, client-
driven improvement plan that is intended to integrate and align all cross-functional
improvement initiatives into a single prioritized plan. The HP helps to identify the
commonalities between the different improvement initiatives and streamline, prioritize
and distribute resources to achieve maximum efficiency.

The Licensing and Compliance project is part of the HP. This project establishes
processes, business requirements, policies, definitions, procedures, work instructions and
forms that describe and support licensing and certification activities conducted by the
Regulatory Operations Branch (ROB). Managing compliance is a core process of
monitoring and enforcing conformity with the regulatory requirements of the NSCA, and
the regulations, licences, certificates and approvals issues under the Act.

The Annual Compliance Report (ACR) is an integral component of verifying compliance.
The ACR process starts when the CNSC sends a reminder letter to the licensee. Once the
licensee writes the report and submits it, the CNSC reviews it. The process concludes
when the information contained in the ACR is saved to the appropriate CNSC data
repositories.

Although different business areas within ROB receive and manage compliance reports,
the Directorate of Nuclear Substance Regulation (DNSR) has been identified as the
business lead for the ACR Online project. As such, the technology solution was
developed based on the DNSR business requirements.

Because implementing the project will involve the collection, use, disclosure and
retention of personal information from Class 2 and NSRD licensees, the CNSC has
initiated a privacy impact assessment (PIA).

Section 2 - Privacy Impact Assessment Findings

This PIA report, produced according to Treasury Board Secretariat guidelines, examines
the privacy-related impacts of the ACR Online initiative and proposes appropriate
mitigation strategies for the identified privacy risks associated with that system.

In particular, the assessment process identifies low risks relating to performance
measurement, indirect collection, notice, breach and the requirement for personal
information banks. Recommendations are provided to mitigate all of these issues.

As the CNSC moves forward with the ACR Online project, privacy principles and fair
information practices, as outlined in both the Standards Council of Canada’s Model Code
for the Protection of Personal Information and the Privacy Act, will continue to be core
program objectives.
