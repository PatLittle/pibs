## Short description of the project, initiative or change

The Enterprise Staffing Team put forward a proposal at a 2019 “Dragons' Den”-style innovation event which ultimately was selected as a winner. The Team proposed an online portal through which to gather information from former students across the public service who are now eligible for student bridging. This is different than other initiatives in the public service as some departments collect and retain information of former students in their respective departments, but not for the public service as a whole. The scope of the project was revised to manage the volume of applicants. Additionally, an internal advertised process open to current students who will graduate by a specific date will be considered. The graduation date will change regularly to ensure a regular intake.

The Student Bridging Portal will allow current students of the public service who are eligible for student bridging, to inform DFO of this fact. Thousands of students are hired each year by the public service, but not all of them become term or indeterminate employees following graduation. This portal gives them a chance to inform DFO that they are currently a student in the public service and allows the organization to capitalize on the investment already made in them once they graduate.

The Student Bridging Portal will feature a user interface that allows for applicants to respond to screening questions and to upload documentation related to their education and achievements. VidCruiter's Applicant Tracking System (“VidTracking”) will allow HR professionals and hiring managers to track and manage applicants using the Student Bridging Portal throughout the recruitment process. The system offers capabilities beyond what the [www.jobs.gc.ca](http://www.jobs.gc.ca) platform provides.

## Risk area identification and categorization

The following section contains risks identified in the PIA for the new or modified program. The numbered risk scale is presented in an ascending order: the first level (1) represents the lowest level of potential risk for the risk area; the fourth level (4) represents the highest level of potential risk for the given risk area.

### Type of program or activity

Administration of program or activity and services - Personal information is used to make decisions that directly affect the individual (i.e. determining eligibility for programs including authentication for accessing programs/services, administering program payments, overpayments, or support to clients, issuing or denial of permits/licenses, processing appeals, etc.)

Level of risk to privacy: Low

### Type of personal information involved and context

Only personal information, with no contextual sensitivities, collected directly from the individual or provided with the consent of the individual for disclosure under an authorized program. (For example the renewal of a license.)

Level of risk to privacy: Low

### Program or activity partners and private sector involvement

Private sector organizations, international organizations or foreign governments. DFO's Student Bridging Portal will be administered by VidCruiter, using its Applicant Tracking System called VidTracking. This private sector involvement elevates the level of risk.

Level of risk to privacy: High

### Duration of the program or activity

Student Bridging Portal using VidCruiter's Applicant Tracking System (VidTracking) is planned to be a long term program.

Level of risk to privacy: High

### Program population

The program will use information of former students employed in the public service who are eligible to be bridged in. The program affects certain individuals for external administrative purposes.

Level of risk to privacy: Medium

### Technology & privacy

The new or substantially modified program or activity involves implementation of a new electronic system or the use of a new application or software, including collaborative software (or groupware), to support the program or activity in terms of the creation, collection or handling of personal information.

Overall risk to privacy: Medium

### Personal information transmission

The personal information is transmitted using wireless technologies. The portal will allow upload of information and retention in the cloud.

Level of risk to privacy: High

### Risk impact to the individual or employee in the event of a privacy breach

The information collected is limited to non-sensitive personal information, and is provided by individuals directly with their knowledge and consent.

Level of risk to privacy: Low

### Risk impact to the institution in the event of a privacy breach

Potential managerial harm. Processes must be reviewed, tools must be changed, change in provider.

Level of risk to privacy: Low
