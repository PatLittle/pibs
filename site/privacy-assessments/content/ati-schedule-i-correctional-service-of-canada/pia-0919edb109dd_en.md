# Express Lane Staffing (ELS)

## Privacy impact assessment (PIA) summary

### Overview

Express Lane is a simple interactive web site for managers that standardizes, simplifies, and expedites our low-risk staffing process and actions requiring limited human resources (HR) consultation. This web-based self-service approach enables managers to place requests online for low-risk staff needs in these categories:

- assignments/secondments without relocation or traveling costs
- casual employment
- external and internal non-advertised deployment without relocation costs
- student employment
- term extensions: extensions beyond 18 months will not be included in order to avoid potential rollover, unrealistic expectations, etc.
- short-term acting appointments (less than four months)
- subsequent acting appointments

### Summary of risks and recommendation

No risks were identified.
