About this data product

Authority, provenance and reuse

Institution membership and legal names come from Schedule I of the Access to Information Act. Treasury Board publication requirements supply annual due dates; Open Government organization data supplies identifiers and metadata. Institution Info Source publications supply the PIB and Class-of-Records content.

The collection preserves dated raw responses, checksums and bilingual normalized tables. Review the collection audit, data model, and methodology. Source code and generated data are available in the GitHub repository under its stated licence.
