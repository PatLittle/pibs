Privacy impact assessment summaries

A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is a risk management tool that focuses on compliance with the Privacy Act, to ensure that privacy implications are appropriately identified, assessed and mitigated when a new or substantially modified program or activity involving personal information is implemented.

DFO is committed to ensuring that privacy protection is a core consideration in the development and administration of programs and activities involving personal information and conducts PIAs in accordance with the TBS Directive on Privacy Impact Assessment.

The following is a list of PIA summaries which have been completed by Fisheries and Oceans Canada:

  • Oyster Licence Retirement Grant Program
  • Occupational Health and Safety Information System – Workplace Compensation Claims Process
  • Possession and Export of Elver Licence Information
  • Open Source Information Collection (OSIC) Initiative
  • FishingBC App and National Recreational Licensing System (NRLS) Integration Initiative
  • Pacific Salmon Strategy Initiative, Initiative 15.2
  • Pacific Salmon Strategy Initiative, Initiative 15.3
  • Pacific Salmon Strategy Initiative, Initiative 15.4
  • Fishery Officer Body Worn Camera (“BWC”) Pilot Project
  • Aquatic Invasive Species Program
  • Atlantic Fisheries Fund
  • Fisheries and Aquaculture Clean Technology Adoption Program
  • Ice Assistance Emergency Program
  • National Fisheries Intelligence Service
  • National Introductions and Transfers Program
  • Student Bridging Portal using VidCruiter’s Applicant Tracking System (VidTracking)
  • SAP S/4HANA - Government of Canada Finance and Materiel Management Solution
