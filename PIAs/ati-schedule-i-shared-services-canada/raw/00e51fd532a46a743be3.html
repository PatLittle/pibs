Publications – Access to information and privacy

On this page, you will find access to information and privacy (ATIP) publications. These include:

  • annual reports to Parliament
  • stakeholder engagement reports (for example, a “What we heard” report)
  • privacy impact assessment (PIA) summaries
  • technology assessment and privacy implications (TAPI) summaries

For more information about these publications, please contact us.

Privacy Impact Assessment summaries

Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA) are a planning tool used by government organizations to assess potential privacy risks in new or changing programs. They support responsible decision making by helping teams identify how personal information will be collected, used and protected.

Technology Assessment for Privacy Implications summaries

The Technology Assessment for Privacy Implications (TAPI) is a templated assessment tool created by SSC to help government organizations identify privacy risks when they plan to use new technologies.
