# Employee Monitoring PIA

## 1. September 2025

### Title

Employee Monitoring PIA

### Description of the program or activity

The Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) plans to initiate individual employee in-office presence monitoring starting on September 1, 2025, with the first reporting cycle scheduled for October 1, 2025.This activity will track and report on the use of access badges to monitor in-office presence against the mandatory onsite requirements for both RE employees and REX employees.

While this activity was already in existence at OSFI, tracking and reporting was disclosed at the aggregate level and complied with the Privacy Guidance Bulletin provided in Treasury Board Secretariat (TBS) Technical Package on the Direction on prescribed presence in the workplace to ensure individuals were de-identified; the revised approach to this activity will identify and report on the in-office presence of individual employees, and would require further actions be taken prior to launching this initiative, if OSFI is to continue observing TBS guidance.

### Why a privacy impact assessment (PIA) was completed

OSFI is committed to following the guidance provided by TBS in its approach to this activity. In May 2024, all heads of Human Resources across the government received a Hybrid Technical Package as part of the TBS Direction on prescribed presence in the workplace, which included privacy guidance. To be compliant with TBS guidance, a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) and Personal Information Banks (PIB) authorizing the collection of personal information for this purpose will be completed. The guidance also stipulates that there is an obligation to submit the PIA to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and TBS prior to the September 1st launch date to permit the opportunity for OPC to review and discuss the issues involved with the roll-out of this activity.

### More information

1. The goal of this activity is to strengthen compliance and oversight by providing people managers with reports as an additional tool, not a definitive source, to identify and validate gaps between in-office presence and onsite requirements for the employees that report to them.
2. The proposed approach of sharing in-office presence data is through a secured dashboard. People managers will have access to the data of the employees underneath them only (for example, the Superintendent will have access to all employee's data), and each individual employee will be able to access their own data.
