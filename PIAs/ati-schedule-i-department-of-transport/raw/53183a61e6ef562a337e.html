Privacy Impact Assessment Summary for the Civil Aviation Licensing Operations Program

Introduction

Civil Aviation Licencing encompasses the following four personal information banks (PIBs):

  1. Civil Aviation Personnel Licensing – Bank No. TC PPU 005: information related to the issuance, validation, monitoring, and control of all Canadian Aviation Documents (i.e. licences and permits) to persons who pilot or control aircraft (aircraft flight crew members, air traffic controllers, and those applying for such licences and permits). The licensing of pilots and air traffic controllers requires the collection of application forms, medical examination information (from TC PPU 020), written examination, and flight test information to determine if a particular licence will be issued. Once issued, and upon application by the individual, the program produces an Aviation Document Booklet (ADB), which resembles a passport with a bio-data page (with photograph), but also includes data on their medical examination, licensing, and any ratings.
  2. Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME) Licensing – Bank No. TC PPU 011: information related to the issuance of an AME licence, which follows a similar application process to pilots and air traffic controllers, but does not require a medical examination. AMEs are individuals who perform mechanical and technical repairs and maintenance on aircraft. AMEs are not issued an ADB; instead, they are issued a licence card (similar to a driver’s licence) which details the types of aircraft they are authorized to certify as airworthy. The AME licence is critical to the Canadian transportation industry because an aircraft cannot legally fly unless a licenced AME has signed off on their maintenance.
  3. Medical Assessments – Bank No. TC PPU 020: this bank and program activity is specific to the medical examinations performed by Certified Aviation Medical Examiners (CAMEs). A CAME is contacted by a pilot, student pilot, or air traffic controller seeking a medical certificate confirming they are medically fit to hold the licence, permit, and rating to which they are seeking from Transport Canada. CAMEs must assess the individual’s medical fitness based on factors laid out in the Canadian Aviation Regulations (CAR).
  4. Civil Aviation Medical Examiners (CAME) – Bank No. TC PPU 031: this bank and program activity are limited to the collection of information on licenced physicians who are delegated by the Minister of Transport for a term of four years to conduct medical examinations of pilots and air traffic controllers. At any given time, there are approximately 500 CAMEs. Over time, a CAME may be renewed or terminated from the program; they can remove themselves or TC may terminate their role based on performance.

Furthermore, to support the processing of licensing applications and the medical assessments required of applicants, the program collects information from the Department of Justice on individuals who are in significant arrears of a support order. The Department of Justice is required under the Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act (FOAEAA) to provide information on those individuals in arrears. In response, Transport Canada is required under FOAEAA to suspend a licence and refuse to issue a new licence until such time that the individual pays off their debt.

Why a privacy impact assessment was completed

A PIA on Civil Aviation Licencing has never been completed by the department. As Transport Canada is modernizing its systems and supporting tools (web forms, mobile apps, and a Digital personnel Licence), the department desired a PIA to assess the baseline privacy risks of the program.

In the future, as the modernization project advances, this PIA or other PIAs will be authored when determined necessary.

PIA risk summary and mitigation strategies

The PIA identified several potential privacy risks associated with the administration of the program, including issues around accountability, notice, retention, transparency, and information security. In response to the risks, senior management within the Civil Aviation Licencing Program created and approved an action plan to mitigate the risks within a timely fashion.

Related personal information bank:

  • Civil Aviation Personnel Licensing (TC PPU 005)
  • Aircraft Maintenance Engineer Licensing (TC PPU 011)
  • Medical Assessments (TC PPU 020)
  • Civil Aviation Medical Examiners (CAME) (TC PPU 031)

For more information on this PIA, please contact services@tc.gc.ca.
