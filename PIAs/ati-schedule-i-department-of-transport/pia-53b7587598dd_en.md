# Privacy Impact Assessment Summary for the Transportation Security Clearance Programs

## Introduction

Transport Canada’s transportation security clearance programs help protect the security of Canada’s aviation, marine, rail and preclearance transportation systems. The programs assess the reliability and suitability of individuals who require unescorted access to restricted areas at transportation facilities or who perform duties that could affect transportation security. Individuals apply for a security clearance by providing personal information that is used to verify their identity and conduct background checks with authorized federal partners. Based on the results of these checks, Transport Canada determines whether an individual is eligible to receive or maintain a transportation security clearance.

## Why a privacy impact assessment was completed

A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) was completed to examine how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, retained, and safeguarded in the administration of the transportation security clearance programs. The PIA was conducted to ensure that the programs comply with the Privacy Act and Treasury Board privacy policy requirements, and to assess how personal information is managed when shared with partner institutions involved in the screening process. The assessment also responds to previous recommendations from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada regarding the privacy protections associated with the programs.

## PIA risk summary and mitigation strategies

The PIA identified several potential privacy risks related to the handling of personal information in the screening process. These include risks associated with sharing personal information with partner institutions involved in background checks, including foreign partners; the potential collection of more personal information than necessary in supporting documentation; and the need to ensure transparency regarding how personal information is used and disclosed. Risk mitigation measures were implemented or are being implemented to address these issues. These measures include strengthening information-sharing agreements to clarify safeguards and permitted uses of personal information, reviewing application processes to support data minimization, improving privacy notices provided to applicants, and enhancing system safeguards such as access controls and audit logging. Implementation of these measures began following completion of the PIA and will continue as agreements and program documentation are updated.
