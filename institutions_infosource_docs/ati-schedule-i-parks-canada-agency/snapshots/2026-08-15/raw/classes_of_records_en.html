Aquatic invasive species prevention permits

Description
Includes information on the movement of watercraft and water-related gear into and around park waters including location of last use, type of watercraft and other water gear, location of next intended use and condition of watercraft and other water gear, for the purpose of protecting aquatic ecosystems from the introduction and spread of aquatic invasive species.
Document types
Permits application forms; restricted activity orders; guidelines and standards of practice; program manuals and standard operating procedures; databases; summary reports and analyses of aggregated data resulting from permit information.
Record number
PC PAEC HRC AIS 001

Claims and Litigation Program

Description
Information resources pertain to incidents and/or accidents that have occurred on Parks Canada sites to make administrative decisions on property damage and personal injuries claims. This also includes information resources related to the operation, maintenance and/or design of Parks Canada’s assets and infrastructures. These functions are guided by key governance instruments, including the Treasury Board’s Framework on the Management of Risk, Guide to Claims and Directive on Payment, the Parks Canada Delegation Matrix, and the Delegation of Spending and Financial Authorities Supporting Notes.
Document types
This bank contains documents such as claim release forms; incident reports; witness statements; photographs and videos; insurance policy documents; investigation notes; legal opinions; settlement policy, and any supporting documentation used by Parks Canada in making administrative decisions related to property damage or personal injury claims. It will also include inspection reports on maintenance of facilities; operation and maintenance standards; maintenance schedules; and safety directives.
Record number
PC TTI THM CP 001

Land use planning activities

Description
This class of records contains documents and information created, collected, and maintained as evidence of activities related to the review and processing of land use planning permits and approval applications. These records relate to the delivery of land use planning services, the application of Parks Canada’s Master List of Fees, the development and implementation of service standards, the permitting process, remissions and associated policies, and the terms and conditions governing land use permits and agreements. The records also include information related to projects undertaken on Parks Canada leased or licensed lands, including project planning, design, construction, and compliance documentation.
Document types
The records include feasibility studies, options analysis documents, application forms, project briefs, design data, cost estimates, project schedules, legal documents, correspondence, technical evaluations, compliance reports, administrative decisions, notices, issued permits, tools and logs supporting the management of projects, tasks, and records. This class of records also includes documentation related to projects undertaken on Parks Canada–leased or licensed lands, including architectural drawings, plans and specifications, leases and licenses, environmental assessments and reports, design and construction documentation, copies of relevant legislation, regulation, policies, and records demonstrating compliance with applicable regulatory and contractual requirements.

Visitors experience research

Description
The Agency creates, collects, and maintains records that serve as evidence of the collection, analysis, and use of visitor related data. The data serve as evidence of how information about visitors is gathered, interpreted, and applied. They provide a document trail of data collection practices, decisions made based on findings. They also document how information is stored, managed, and retained within the branch responsible for visitor, market, and public opinion research.
Document types
Surveys responses, data files, intercept logs; research instruments; questionnaires; moderator guides, interview guides; planning documents; analytical reports, infographics, and summaries. They also include records of methodologies used; data quality checks; and version control documentation.
