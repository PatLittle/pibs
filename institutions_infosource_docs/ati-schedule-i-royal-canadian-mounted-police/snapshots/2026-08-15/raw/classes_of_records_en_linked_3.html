The RCMP produces forms in Portable Document Format, which are fillable and savable for your convenience.

Filling and saving PDF forms

To be able to fill in and save a PDF form, download and then open the form using the free Adobe Reader:

  1. Save the PDF fillable or savable form in a folder that you will easily find on your computer.
  2. Launch Adobe Reader.
  3. Browse to the folder where you saved the file, and open it from within Adobe Reader. You can now fill and save your PDF form.

Note

RCMP fillable or savable PDF forms have been tested for compatibility with Adobe Reader 9 and higher and may not be fully supported in other PDF viewers and on some mobile devices.

Consent forms for use by accredited fingerprint companies and police agencies

RCMP 6673 Consent for Certified Criminal Record Checks - Employment Purposes [PDF - 224 KB]

Use this form if you are applying for paid or unpaid employment with no authority or trust over a vulnerable person.

For use by accredited fingerprint companies and police agencies for Certified Criminal Record Check applications (i.e., fingerprint-based criminal record check; not a name-based criminal record check).

Do not send your completed forms to Canadian Criminal Real Time Identification Services unless you have received a request.

Fee remission

Form number Form title Notes
RCMP 6599 Confirmation of Eligibility for Fee Waiver: Reclaimed Name Under Truth and Reconciliation Commission Call to Action 17 [PDF - 177 KB]

Use this form if you are an Indigenous person who is reclaiming your Indigenous name, and you are required by your province or territory to get a fingerprint-based criminal record check.

This form must be submitted at the time of fingerprinting for a criminal record verification to have the $25 administrative fee waived.

Managing criminal record information

Form number Form title Notes
RCMP 3953 Request to Remove Adult Absolute and/or Conditional Discharge Sentence prior to July 24, 1992 [PDF - 179 KB]

Use this form if you were given a discharge sentence prior to July 24, 1992.

If you received a discharge sentence on or after July 24, 1992, your information is automatically removed from Canadian Police Information Centre. However, per Section 6.2 of the Criminal Records Act your discharge sentence will be held separate and apart in the National Repository of Criminal Records for identification purposes.
