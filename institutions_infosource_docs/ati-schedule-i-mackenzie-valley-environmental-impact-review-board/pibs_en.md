# Access to Information and Personal Information Requests

The Mackenzie Valley Environmental Impact Review Board is an independent, public board funded by the federal government. Its governing legislation is the Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act and the Review Board is subject to the *Access to Information Act* and the *Privacy Act.*

If you would like to submit a formal **Access to Information Request** form please download the form:

* [English form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-57-nf-eng.pdf)
* [French form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-57-nf-fra.pdf)

If you are not satisfied with our response to your access to information request, you may wish to contact the [Office of the Information Commissioner of Canada.](http://www.oic-ci.gc.ca/eng/)

If you would like to submit a formal **Personal Information Request** from please download the form:

* [English Form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-58-nf-eng.pdf)
* [French Form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-58-nf-fra.pdf)

If you are not satisfied with our response to your privacy request, you may wish to [contact the federal Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.priv.gc.ca/index_e.asp).

Information About Programs and Information Holdings (formerly known as Info Source: Sources of Federal Government and Employee Information) provides information about the functions, programs, activities and related information holdings of government institutions subject to the Access to Information Act and the Privacy Act. It provides individuals and employees of the government (current and former), with relevant information to access personal information about themselves held by government institutions subject to the Privacy Act and to exercise their rights under the Privacy Act.
