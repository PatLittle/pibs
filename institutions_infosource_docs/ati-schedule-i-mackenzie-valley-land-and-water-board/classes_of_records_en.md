## Access to Information Requests

The Mackenzie Valley Land and Water Board is an independent, public board funded by the Federal Government. Its governing legislation is the Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act (MVRMA) and the Board is subject to the federal Access to Information and Privacy Acts.

#### Submitting a request online to Mackenzie Valley Land and Water Board

The Access to Information and Privacy Online Request Service offers convenient way to submit access to information or personal information requests.

This service enables individuals to make online requests for information under the control of Government of Canada institutions instead of having to print, scan and email or mail a form to the correct institution. All ATIP related questions should be directed to the [ATIP Coordinator](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#770012151a1604031205371a011b00155914181a).

* [Submit request online](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/request-information.html)

#### Soumettre une demande en ligne à Mackenzie Valley Land and Water Board

Le Service de demandes d’AIPRP en ligne offre un moyen pratique de présenter des demandes d’accès à l’information ou à des renseignements personnels.

Ce service permet aux particuliers de présenter des demandes en ligne de renseignements sous le contrôle des institutions du gouvernement du Canada au lieu d’avoir à imprimer, à numériser et à envoyer un formulaire par courriel ou par la poste à l’institution pertinente.

* [Soumettre la demande en ligne](https://atip-aiprp.apps.gc.ca/atip/privacyTerms.do)

If you would like to submit an **Access to Information Request** form please download the form:

* [English form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-57-nf-eng.pdf)
* [French form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-57-nf-fra.pdf)

If you are not satisfied with our response to your Access to Information Request, you may wish to contact the [Office of the Information Commissioner of Canada](http://www.oic-ci.gc.ca/eng/).

If you would like to submit a **Personal Information Request** from please download the form:

* [English Form](https://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-58-nf-eng.pdf)
* [French Form](http://www.tbs-sct.gc.ca/tbsf-fsct/350-58-nf-fra.pdf)

If you are not satisfied with our response to your Privacy Request, you may wish to [contact the federal Office of the Privacy Commissioner](http://www.priv.gc.ca/index_e.asp).

The Mackenzie Valley Land and Water Board posts summaries of completed Access to Information Requests. MVLWB began posting summaries of completed Access to Information Requests in January, 2012.
