## Internal Services

Internal services are groups of related activities and resources that are administered to support the needs of programs and other corporate obligations of an organization. These groups are: [Acquisitions](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#acq); [Communications Services](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#comm); [Financial Management](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#fm); [Human Resources Management](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#hrm); [Information Management](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#im); [Information Technology](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#it); [Legal Services](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#ls); [Management and Oversight Services](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#mos); [Material](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#mat); [Real Property](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#rp); [Travel and Other Administrative Services](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/access-information-privacy/access-information/info-source/internal-services.html#toas). Internal Services include only those activities and resources that apply across an organization and not to those provided specifically to a program.

Communications Services involve activities undertaken to ensure that Government of Canada communications are effectively managed, well-coordinated and responsive to the diverse information needs of the public.
