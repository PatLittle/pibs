Agent Affairs

Description: Includes records related to the management of legal agents hired by the PPSC to conduct prosecutions on behalf of the Federal Crown where the PPSC does not have a regional office or where it is impractical or otherwise not cost-effective for staff counsel to handle cases. In addition, there are records pertaining to the application, selection and agreement processes for legal agents, as well as the rules and guidelines that govern the agents' relationship with the PPSC and other federal government departments or enforcement agencies who refer matters for prosecution.

Document Types: Submissions, selection criteria, curricula vitae, application for Fix-Term Agreement, correspondence, Terms and Conditions of Fixed-Term Agreements of Agents, Fixed-Term Agreements, reference check procedures, rating assessments, interview questions, certification, criminal prosecution fee schedule.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 002

Anti-terrorism

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of terrorism-related offences under the anti-terrorism provisions of the Criminal Code and the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act. In addition, there are records pertaining to the work of Canada's Integrated National Security Enforcement Teams (INSETs).

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative and intelligence reports, research material, correspondence, draft court submissions, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information , database of court decisions on terrorism and non-terrorism cases relevant to terrorism prosecutions, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements, documents pertaining to proceeds of crime and property subject to forfeiture.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 020

Competition Law

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of offences under the Competition Act as well as the Consumer Packaging and Labelling Act, the Textile Labelling Act, and the Precious Metals Marking Act. These statutes are administered and enforced by the Commissioner of Competition, who overseas the Competition Bureau. They also contain legal advice provided by PPSC prosecutors during the course of investigations on matters that may lead to prosecutions.

Document Types: Memorandum of Understanding, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative reports, research material, correspondence, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, transcripts, assessments, invoices, accused statements.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 010

Drug Prosecutions

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of offences under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act. The records contain information relating to a broad range of criminal activity, including possession of marihuana, cocaine trafficking, and the production of methamphetamine for export. In addition, there are records involving the operations of Drug Treatment Courts that have been established in major urban centres to help reduce recidivism by addressing the problem posed by addiction-motivated crime. They contain information relating to the work of prosecutors, judges, defence counsel, treatment providers and other personnel who provide supervision and support for participants admitted into the program.

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative reports, research material, correspondence, draft court submissions, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements, documents pertaining to proceeds of crime and property subject to forfeiture.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 001

Economic Crime Prosecutions

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of offences under all statutes administered by the Canada Revenue Agency, including the Income Tax Act and the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act. The records also include prosecution-related advice and litigation support to federal investigative agencies investigating offences under the Income Tax Act, the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act and capital market fraud offences under the Criminal Code.

Document Types: Agreements, notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative reports, research material, correspondence, financial reports and evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, income tax returns, notice of assessments.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 005

Federal-Provincial-Territorial (FPT) Heads of Prosecutions Committee

Description: Includes records related to the Federal-Provincial-Territorial (FPT) Heads of Prosecutions Committee's role in coordinating many aspects of criminal law practice across Canada, including promoting the assistance and cooperation among different prosecution services and facilitating the coordination and adoption, where possible, of a consistent position on national prosecution issues. In addition, there are records relating the Director of Public Prosecutions’ role as permanent co-chair of the Committee as well as the PPSC's role as its secretariat.

Document Types: Committee records (including Committee terms of reference, list of members, agendas, records of decisions, recommendations and minutes), notices, copies of best practices, precedents and guidelines, research material, copies of Committee policies and procedures, studies and reports, and general correspondence.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 004

Fine Recovery

Description: Includes records related to the administration of the National Fine Recovery Program under the terms of an assignment issued by the Attorney General of Canada in 2007. The records pertain to the outstanding fines that are recovered through various types of interventions, including demand letters, telephone contacts, payment negotiations, seizure of assets, and income garnishment. They can also be recovered through the suspension of federal licenses and by way of set-off against the offender's income tax and GST. The final resort is the issuance of warrant of committal in cases where offenders try to avoid making payment despite their ability to pay.

Document Types: Notes, correspondence, court orders, financial records, payment information, agreements with Canada Revenue Agency, descriptions of seized assets.

Disclosure Summaries:

  • GENERAL CREDIT SERVICES INC. fine collection services contract, effective as of March 12, 2024
  • FINANCIAL DEBT RECOVERY LTD fine collection services contract, effective as of September 22, 2023
  • GATESTONE & CO fine collection services contract, effective as of October 4, 2023

Record Number: PPSC REG 001

Organized Crime

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of organized crime offences. In addition, the records consist of legal advice provided by PPSC prosecutors to the RCMP and other law enforcement agencies during their investigations.

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative and intelligence reports, research material, correspondence, draft court submissions, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, database of court decisions on organized crime and non-organized cases relevant to organized crimes prosecutions, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements, documents pertaining to proceeds of crime and property subject to forfeiture.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 005

Proceeds of Crime and Offence-Related Property

Description: Includes records related to the seizure and restraint of proceeds of crime as well as property used in the commission of offences. As drug offences are often revenue-generating crimes, they represent the majority of offences that generate proceeds of crime and property used to commit crime. There are also records pertaining to the Integrated Proceeds of Crime (IPOC) initiative, which contributes to the disruption and deterrence of targeted organized criminal groups by pursuing their illegally obtained proceeds and assets.

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, investigative and intelligence reports, research material, correspondence, descriptions of seized property, draft court submissions , forensic and documentary evidence, witness list, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements, documents pertaining to proceeds of crime and property subject to forfeiture.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 010

Prosecutions in Canada's North

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of all Criminal Code offences and other federal offences in the Northwest Territories, Nunavut and the Yukon. The records also contain information pertaining to the special training, recruitment and retention needs in the territories. In addition, there are records pertaining to the Crown Witness Coordinators who work to support victims and witnesses during their involvement with the criminal justice system as well as records pertaining to the Northern Victim Services Coordinator whose role it is to support and enhance Crown Witness Coordination services in the territories.

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative and intelligence reports, research material, correspondence, draft court submissions, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, medical and laboratory reports, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements, documents pertaining to proceeds of crime and property subject to forfeiture, drafts and final copies of training materials, lecture notes and presentations, course registration procedures, recruitment policies and guidelines.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 015

Regulatory Prosecutions

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of offences that are aimed at protecting the health, safety and general welfare of the public and the environment. They include offences under statutes such as the Fisheries Act, the Immigration and Refugee Act, the Canada Elections Act, the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, the Food and Drugs Act, the Copyright Act, the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act and the Canada Labour Code.

Document Types: Agreements, notes, legal opinions, investigative reports, research material, correspondence, environmental and ecological assessments, studies, expert reports, Crown briefs, , forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, accused statements, regulatory documentation.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 006

School for Prosecutors

Description: Includes records related to the administration and management of the School for Prosecutors which offers annual in-house training aimed at promoting professional development relevant to the prosecution function. The course material would include information on the role of the prosecutor, trial advocacy, wiretap issues, search and seizure, informer privilege, expert evidence, disclosure issues, proceeds of crime, regulatory offences, and preventing miscarriages of justice. In addition, there are records relating to course development and delivery, faculty and guest lecturers, operational policies and procedures, training materials and course registration.

Document Types: Training materials (including lecture notes and presentations), lists of faculty and guest lecturers, course administration records, and registration procedures.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC REG 003

War Crimes and Crimes Against Humanity

Description: Includes records related to the prosecution of offences under the Crimes Against Humanity and War Crimes Act.

Document Types: Notes, legal opinions, Crown briefs, investigative and intelligence reports, research material, correspondence, research memoranda, draft court submissions, documents obtained from foreign countries, forensic and documentary evidence, witness lists, witnesses’ notes and statements, exhibits, expert reports, certificates, search warrants/production orders, arrest and search information, sentencing information, photographs, videos/audio recordings, transcripts, surveillance and wiretap evidence, accused statements.

Disclosure Summaries: Under development

Record Number: PPSC LGL 025
